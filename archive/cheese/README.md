## Archived calculators

These Tower, Boss, Trial and Halloween cheese pages were taken out of the live Depths-only website in v29. They are kept here for future restoration, not loaded by the production interface.

The existing combat engine, card data, and Depths simulator remain in the active source tree. To restore an archived page later, move its scripts and styles back into `src/`, reconnect them in `index-base.html`, and reinstate the appropriate tests.
